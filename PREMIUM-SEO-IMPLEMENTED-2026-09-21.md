# বাংলা সংবাদ — Premium SEO Layer

Implemented without changing the site's ads, social automation, sharing UI, image loader, or page layout.

## Included
- Article-specific title and meta description generated from headline/details/category.
- Canonical URLs for homepage, category pages, and articles.
- NewsArticle JSON-LD with publisher, author, datePublished/dateModified, images, keywords, language and free-access signals.
- Open Graph + X/Twitter article metadata.
- Organization + WebSite + WebPage/CollectionPage JSON-LD for public pages.
- Complete XML sitemap covering public pages and generated news URLs.
- Google News sitemap namespace and publication metadata for recent articles.
- Existing Google Sheet → generator workflow remains the source of future article SEO.

## Important
Structured data improves machine understanding but does not guarantee rankings or Google News inclusion. Validate deployed pages in Google Search Console and Rich Results Test after upload.
